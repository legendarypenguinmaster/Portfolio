01 — The studio

A software development team for freelance work.

We pursue US jobs as one studio. Client partners own the relationship and the freelance account. Engineering owns planning, build, and delivery. Project fees are shared 30% / 70%.

02 — Two roles

Each seat has a clear owner.

Client operations

30%

Client partners

  • Work from a legitimate freelance account in their own name — Upwork, Handshake, LinkedIn, and similar.
  • Handle day-to-day client communication: relaying messages and sharing updates.
  • Receive drafted technical replies from the delivery team whenever needed.
  • Collect payment on the account, then share project fees with the studio.

The client side is a relationship seat. Coding is not part of the job.

Engineering

70%

Delivery team

  • Source suitable projects and handle bidding.
  • Own technical planning, development, testing, and delivery support.
  • Keep work on schedule and up to the studio standard.
  • Stay accountable for quality so partners are not guessing at the build.

US client work is the target. The build stays inside the studio.

04 — What we ship

  • Product features

  • Web software

    Interfaces, application logic, and the supporting pieces a US client expects finished.

  • Integrations

    APIs, data handoff, and the practical connections a project needs to go live.

  • Delivery support

    Fixes, handoff notes, and follow-through after the first version ships.
